Allowance. Yard work. A summer job. Twenty dollars a month.

Most of that money ends up somewhere like this. A jar. A drawer. An account
that does nothing. It just sits there, and twenty dollars a month stays twenty
dollars a month.

But put the same twenty somewhere it can grow, and something different starts
happening.

Year one, almost nothing. Two hundred and fifty dollars, versus two hundred
and forty. Ten dollars apart.

Year five, still small. Year ten, the gap opens up.

And then it really starts to move. Because the growth is growing too.

Thirty years. The same twenty dollars a month, every month.

In a jar, seventy-two hundred dollars. Invested, around twenty-four thousand.

You put in the same money either way. About seventeen thousand of that is
growth.

Twenty dollars a month. Time does the heavy lifting.

Compliance: the script never says "you will have" or "you'll earn". Every
figure is stated as what the illustration shows. The video carries the
"hypothetical 7% average annual growth rate" label on screen for the whole
chart section and on the end card, so the narration does not need to repeat
it — but if you want it spoken, the cleanest place is after the year-30 line:

  "…every month, at a hypothetical seven percent average annual growth rate."

That adds roughly 2.5 s, so stretch LAPSE_STEPS or trim the year 5 / year 10
line if you add it.

Delivery: let the silences sit. There is deliberate space at 3.4–4.2 (scene
change), 15.0–16.0 (year-1 beat), 25.5–28.0 (the curve accelerating) and
31.0–32.4 (the chart settling). Those gaps are where the animation does the
talking.

Voice: something warm and conversational rather than newsreader. A slightly
lower pitch on "It just sits there" and a lift on "something different starts
happening" gives the piece its turn.
